The file data/deceptive-opinion.csv contains the Deceptive Opinion Spam corpus
of M. Ott, Y. Choi, C. Cardie, and J. T. Hancock (ACL-HLT 2011) and
M. Ott, C. Cardie, and J. T. Hancock (NAACL-HLT 2013), in the CSV format
distributed on Kaggle (https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rtatman/deceptive-opinion-spam-corpus).
It is redistributed unchanged.
